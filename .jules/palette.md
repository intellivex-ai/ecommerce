## 2024-05-15 - Product Card Keyboard Accessibility
**Learning:** Hover-revealed UI elements using Tailwind's `group-hover:opacity-100` are completely inaccessible to keyboard navigation by default. They require `focus-within:opacity-100` on the container or `focus-visible:opacity-100` on the child to ensure they appear when focused via Tab.
**Action:** Always pair `group-hover:opacity-100` with `focus-within:opacity-100` when the container includes interactive elements, and ensure icon-only buttons receive an explicit `aria-label` and `focus-visible` ring.
